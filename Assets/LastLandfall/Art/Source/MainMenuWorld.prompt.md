# Main menu world artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The selected artwork is copied to `Assets/LastLandfall/Resources/UI/MainMenuWorld.png` and used by the animated title screen. It represents the whole flooded world with multiple island maps. Typography and controls are rendered separately by Unity.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final background art for a PC game MAIN TITLE SCREEN that represents an ENTIRE WORLD with MANY different selectable island maps. Wide 16:9 cinematic landscape. IMPORTANT COMPOSITION: DO NOT showcase one foreground island, DO NOT make a single island the main subject, DO NOT repeat the specific current map. The main subject is the immense flooded world: a huge expanse of cold dark teal ocean stretching to a distant slightly curved luminous horizon, many SEPARATE scattered groups of surviving mountain islands across the middle and right distance. Between all island groups there must be very broad open ocean channels; the composition reads as an archipelagic world awaiting expeditions, not as a close aerial screenshot of a level. High aerial panoramic viewpoint, restrained illustrated map-like readability without any map labels or UI. Islands have varied believable landforms: a distant broad elevated plateau group, a broken chain of jagged mountain summits, a low fractured rock archipelago, a long ridgeline emerging from floodwater. All island groups are relatively small within the vast seascape. Scale hierarchy: ocean and atmospheric sky about 85 percent, dispersed exposed land about 15 percent, no dominant central hero island. Left third is dark quiet negative space of ocean under heavy blue-gray clouds, for separate game title and menu. Light breaks through thinning storm clouds far upper right, a thin pale warm dawn path on the sea, deep layered blue atmospheric perspective, bands of slow sea fog and silver cloud shadows. The story is a global flood that submerged continents; new journeys and settlements may happen on many different surviving islands. Tone of isolation, uncertainty, hope, exploration, rebuilding across a large world. Premium realistic painterly game key art, beautifully resolved subtle ocean waves and far rocky mountain silhouettes; elegant and cinematic, not tropical vacation. No lettering, no game logo, no buttons, no overlays, no compass, no watermark. No beaches or sand. No vegetation, no trees, no buildings, no boats, no people. Do not create an enclosed circular crater or basin on a summit. Make this a WORLD OVERVIEW BACKGROUND, never a close image of a specific island.

## Runtime animation

Slow image drift and zoom, localized subtle ocean ripple/reflections and drifting procedural mist. The settings page can freeze the background. The title screen fades into the live game when starting or resuming.
